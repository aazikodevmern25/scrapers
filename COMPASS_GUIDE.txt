========================================
  METHOD 1: Navigate in Compass
========================================

1. Open MongoDB Compass (already open in your screenshot)

2. In LEFT PANEL (CONNECTIONS section):
   - Scroll through database list
   - Find "Dhruval" (alphabetically around D-E section)
   - Click the ▶ arrow next to "Dhruval"
   - Click "trademap" collection
   - View your 6 documents!

========================================
  METHOD 2: Use Search Filter
========================================

1. At top of left panel, find "Search connections" box
2. Type: Dhruval
3. Press Enter
4. It will show only Dhruval database
5. Expand and click "trademap"

========================================
  METHOD 3: Direct Connection
========================================

1. Click "+ Add Connection" button in Compass
2. Enter connection string:
   mongodb://localhost:27017/Dhruval
3. Click "Connect"
4. Click "trademap" collection

========================================
  TROUBLESHOOTING
