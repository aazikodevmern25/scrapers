# MacMap Trade Agreements Scraper

## Form URL
https://5060e3a58fb3.ngrok-free.app/trade-agreements-form

## API Endpoints

### Single Country
POST https://5060e3a58fb3.ngrok-free.app/api/v1/scrape/trade-agreements
Content-Type: application/json
Body: {"country": "China"}

### Multiple Countries
POST https://5060e3a58fb3.ngrok-free.app/api/v1/scrape/trade-agreements/bulk
Content-Type: application/json
Body: {"countries": ["China", "India", "Japan"]}

## How to Use

1. Open the form URL in your browser
2. Select countries from the dropdown or enter comma-separated names
3. Click "Generate Payload"
4. Tasks will be created and processed
5. Data appears in MongoDB as FTAs are found (real-time)
